DETAILED DESCRIPTION — COPY THE TEXT BELOW

Go from an alphaXiv paper to its arXiv page in one click. Back to arXiv adds a small page button, a toolbar popup, and an action for paper links in Chrome's right-click menu.

Choose how you read
• Open the arXiv abstract page, PDF, or HTML (experimental).
• Use the current tab or open a new one with the page button, popup, or shortcut.
• Save your reading format and paper-version preference once and use them across the extension.

Open a paper straight from a link
Right-click a supported alphaXiv or arXiv paper link on another webpage and choose Open in arXiv. Your selected format and version open in a new tab, keeping the source page available.

Choose the current or latest version
Current version is the default: a link containing v3 keeps v3 when you open or copy it. Choose Latest version to generate an unversioned link that arXiv resolves to its latest paper version. A source link without a version stays unversioned. Modern and legacy arXiv identifiers are supported.

Copy the link you need
The main Copy original link button copies the arXiv abstract-page link. Use the adjacent arrow menu in the popup or on the page to copy an Abstract, PDF, or HTML link. Each copy action uses your selected version preference and leaves your default reading format unchanged. Generated links keep the paper identifier and remove source tracking parameters and discussion anchors.

Use a shortcut
Press Alt+Shift+A on Windows/Linux or Option+Shift+A on macOS to open the current paper. Customize the assigned shortcut through the popup or Chrome's extension shortcut settings. The popup and shortcut also work on supported arXiv paper pages.

English and Simplified Chinese
The interface starts in English. Choose English, 简体中文, or Follow browser in the popup. Language changes also update the page controls and right-click menu.

Simple, local preferences
Reading format, version, tab-opening, and language preferences are saved in your browser. The extension converts paper URLs locally; opening a paper takes you directly to arXiv. Start by pinning Back to arXiv and opening an alphaXiv paper, or right-click a paper link.

HTML availability depends on the paper and version supported by arXiv. Abstract and PDF remain available as reading choices. Back to arXiv is an independent utility for research reading.
